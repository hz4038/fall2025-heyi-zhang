Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior coding experience?

No! Our "Foundations" track assumes zero prior knowledge. We teach C# from the ground up specifically for game development context.

We recommend dedicating 5-8 hours per week to complete the 8-week curriculum on time. However, all content is self-paced.

Yes, the Unity Personal Edition is completely free for individuals and small teams making under $100k revenue per year.
